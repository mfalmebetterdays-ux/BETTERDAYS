1.0 Background Information

Ikumbi Tea is one of the tea factories under the Kenya Tea Development Agency (KTDA), located in Murang'a County. The factory is known for producing high-quality Kenyan tea and has built a strong reputation within the tea sector over the years. While the factory has been well known within the tea industry, its digital presence remained largely untapped, limiting its visibility to consumers beyond its traditional markets.

To address this gap, a comprehensive social media campaign was initiated to introduce Ikumbi Tea as a consumer brand, increase its online visibility and prepare the market ahead of the official product launch. The campaign focuses on creating awareness, building a strong online community and positioning Ikumbi Tea as a brand that people can relate to in their everyday lives.

Accounts created 24 June 2026
Publishing commenced 3 July 2026

2.0 Introduction & 3.0 Rationale and Context

Introduction: The Ikumbi Tea social media campaign officially began with the creation of all social media accounts on 24th June 2026, while regular content publishing commenced on 3rd July 2026. Since then, the campaign has been implemented across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X with the primary objective of introducing the Ikumbi Tea brand to the market before product launch.

Unlike established brands that already have an audience, Ikumbi Tea started with zero online visibility. The campaign therefore focused on creating awareness first before pushing for sales. Every piece of content published has been designed to make consumers recognize the brand, interact with it and eventually develop enough trust to become future customers once the product is launched.

Within a few weeks, the campaign has successfully transformed Ikumbi Tea from a largely unknown consumer brand into one that is attracting thousands of views, increasing engagement and growing recognition both within Kenya and internationally.

Rationale and Context: The decision to launch the social media campaign before the product launch was deliberate. Brands that are introduced to the market without first creating awareness often struggle to attract customers because people naturally buy products they already know, trust and remember.

The campaign was therefore designed to first build familiarity with the Ikumbi Tea name before introducing the product itself. Instead of asking people to buy immediately, the focus has been on making Ikumbi Tea part of everyday conversations through content that is relatable, timely and enjoyable to watch.

At the beginning of the campaign, Ikumbi Tea had virtually no online recognition beyond Murang'a County. There was no active digital community, very little public awareness and no meaningful engagement across social media platforms. Today, that position has changed significantly.

International reach: Saudi Arabia, Qatar, UAE, Tanzania, Australia, Pakistan, Bahrain, USA.
Current focus: building anticipation so that once the product is officially launched, the campaign shifts from generating attention to converting that attention into actual purchases.

4.0 Objectives of the Campaign

The overall objective of the campaign has been to transform Ikumbi Tea from an unknown digital brand into a recognizable household name before product launch. Every activity undertaken has contributed towards this goal.

The specific objectives were to:

  • Establish and manage official social media platforms for Ikumbi Tea.
  • Introduce the Ikumbi Tea brand to Kenyan consumers and build nationwide awareness.
  • Create content that is relatable to different lifestyles, professions and everyday experiences.
  • Build an active online community through consistent engagement and interaction.
  • Generate curiosity and anticipation ahead of the official product launch.
  • Increase traffic to the brand's social media platforms while expanding audience reach.
  • Position Ikumbi Tea as a modern Kenyan tea brand that consumers can easily relate to.
  • Build a strong digital audience that can later be converted into customers through product launch campaigns.

5.0 Implementation Progress

The implementation of the Ikumbi Tea social media campaign has followed a structured approach aimed at gradually introducing the brand, building awareness, creating audience engagement and preparing consumers for the official product launch. Every stage of the campaign has been guided by one simple principle: make people know the brand first, keep them interested and build enough trust that they become future customers.

Although the campaign is still in its early stages, the progress made within a short period demonstrates that the strategy adopted is working. The growth recorded across all social media platforms confirms that the brand is steadily gaining visibility and attracting attention from both local and international audiences.

5.1 Campaign Concept The concept behind the campaign was to introduce Ikumbi Tea as more than just another tea brand. Rather than focusing only on the product, the campaign aimed at connecting Ikumbi Tea with everyday life. The idea was simple. Every Kenyan has moments that deserve a cup of tea whether at work, in school, at home, on the farm, during celebrations or while simply taking a break. Instead of talking about tea every day, we focused on making Ikumbi Tea part of those moments. This approach allowed the brand to fit naturally into people's lifestyles while making the content feel less like advertising and more like everyday experiences that audiences could relate to. As a result, audiences did not simply watch the content. They interacted with it, shared it and related it to their own daily lives.

5.2 Campaign Strategy The strategy adopted throughout the campaign has been centred on relatable thematic content. Rather than posting random content, every week has been guided by a specific theme based on what people are currently experiencing or talking about. This has allowed the campaign to remain relevant while keeping audiences interested. The campaign began by creating awareness around the Ikumbi Tea brand before gradually introducing more engaging content. During the cold month of July, the "Kick the Cold with Ikumbi" campaign positioned tea as a comforting companion during Kenya's cold season. As the campaign progressed, content evolved into profession-based storytelling under themes that celebrated different occupations and everyday lifestyles. Teachers, farmers, boda boda riders, office professionals, students, health workers, mechanics and many other professions were featured to demonstrate that Ikumbi Tea is for everyone. This strategy proved highly effective because audiences could easily identify with the content. Many viewers shared the posts with colleagues, friends and family members who worked in the featured professions, significantly increasing the campaign's organic reach. Humour was also incorporated into selected content pieces to increase relatability and encourage audience participation. Educational content explaining tea culture and lifestyle-focused content were blended with entertaining posts to ensure the campaign remained balanced and engaging.

5.3 Content Development Content development has been one of the strongest pillars of the campaign. Every piece of content published has been carefully planned to achieve a specific objective rather than simply filling the posting schedule. The content has been developed around four main categories: Brand Awareness Content (introduced the brand identity), Lifestyle Content (integrated into everyday routines), Educational Content (informed about tea culture), and Entertainment Content (light-hearted posts to increase engagement). The combination of these four categories has enabled the campaign to maintain consistency while reaching different audience interests across all platforms.

5.4 Setting Up Digital Assets A strong digital foundation was established before content publishing began. Official social media accounts were successfully created on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X on 24th June 2026. Each platform was professionally branded using consistent profile images, cover photos, colour schemes and messaging to create one unified Ikumbi Tea identity. Publishing officially commenced on 3rd July 2026, following completion of platform optimisation.

5.5 Community Engagement and Audience Interaction Audience engagement has remained a key focus throughout implementation. Rather than simply publishing content, considerable effort has been directed towards interacting with audiences through comments, reactions and discussions across all active platforms. Every interaction has been viewed as an opportunity to strengthen the relationship between the brand and its growing online community. One of the most encouraging observations has been the increasing curiosity surrounding the Ikumbi Tea brand. Since the commencement of the campaign, numerous followers have commented on posts asking when the product will officially be launched, where it will be available and how they can purchase it. These enquiries indicate that the campaign has successfully generated anticipation well ahead of the product entering the market. The profession-based campaigns have attracted particularly strong engagement. Many users have tagged friends, family members and colleagues who could relate to the featured content, resulting in increased organic sharing and wider audience reach. This type of interaction has enabled the campaign to grow naturally while strengthening the connection between the brand and its target audience. The warm reception received across all platforms demonstrates that the content is resonating with audiences and that Ikumbi Tea is gradually becoming a familiar and recognisable brand within the digital space.

7.0 Recommendations and Way Forward

The campaign has successfully achieved its primary objective of introducing the Ikumbi Tea brand into the digital space and building a growing online community ahead of product launch. The level of engagement, increasing audience reach and positive reception indicate that the market is ready for the next phase of the campaign. The focus now shifts from building awareness to converting the attention generated into measurable sales while maintaining the momentum already achieved.

7.1 Product Launch Campaign The official product launch should be supported by a coordinated digital campaign across all active platforms. A structured countdown should be introduced before launch, followed by product reveal content, launch-day coverage and continuous demonstrations showing the product in everyday use. Every launch post should direct consumers to the available purchase channels through clear and consistent calls to action. The objective is to convert the anticipation already created into immediate customer action.

7.2 Conversion Focus The campaign has already generated strong awareness and audience interest. The next priority is converting this traffic into paying customers. Every high-performing post should direct audiences to Facebook Shop, Instagram Shop, WhatsApp Business and the official website, making it as easy as possible for interested consumers to complete a purchase. Consumers who have previously viewed content, interacted with posts or visited the purchase channels should continue receiving follow-up content designed to encourage them to complete their purchase. The success of the campaign moving forward will be measured not only by reach and engagement but by enquiries received, orders placed, repeat purchases and overall sales generated through social media.

7.3 PR Merchandise Campaign To sustain audience excitement after product launch, branded merchandise should be introduced as part of the campaign.

Corporate Collection

Rather than simply distributing these items, they should become part of the campaign itself. Consumers should be encouraged to participate in activities such as Follow and Win, Like and Share, Comment and Win, Quiz Challenges, Tag a Friend campaigns and User Generated Content competitions. Recipients should also be encouraged to share photographs and videos while unboxing their PR packages, wearing the branded merchandise or using the promotional items in their everyday activities. This approach allows one giveaway to generate multiple pieces of content while exposing the Ikumbi Tea brand to entirely new audiences through the participants' own social media platforms.

7.4 Physical Activations As the product enters the market, digital activities should be complemented by carefully planned physical activations. Launch events, exhibitions, roadshows and community engagements should be fully documented through photography and videography before being shared across all social media platforms. This creates authentic content while demonstrating that the Ikumbi Tea brand exists beyond digital platforms. Physical activations will also provide opportunities to collect customer testimonials, interviews and live consumer reactions that can be repurposed into future marketing campaigns.

7.5 Community Building The community already established should continue receiving consistent, relatable and engaging content. Profession-based campaigns, seasonal themes, humour, educational content and lifestyle storytelling should remain central to the content strategy as they have proven effective in attracting strong audience engagement. Customer comments, questions and direct messages should continue receiving timely responses to strengthen trust and maintain positive relationships with the growing online community. Satisfied customers should also be encouraged to share their own experiences with Ikumbi Tea, creating authentic content that strengthens the credibility of the brand.

7.6 Performance Monitoring Performance monitoring should remain a continuous process throughout the campaign. Campaign reviews should continue every two weeks to identify the strongest performing content, highest engaging platforms and the audiences responding most positively to the campaign. Future content, advertising investment and promotional activities should continue being informed by analytics rather than assumptions. This approach ensures that campaign decisions remain data-driven while maximizing return on investment.

9.0 Conclusion

Conclusion: Within a relatively short implementation period, the Ikumbi Tea social media campaign has successfully transformed the brand from having virtually no digital presence into one that is steadily attracting attention both locally and internationally. The campaign has established official social media platforms, developed a consistent content strategy, built an engaged online community and generated growing curiosity ahead of the official product launch. Audience engagement, organic reach, increasing brand searches and expanding international visibility demonstrate that the campaign is achieving its intended objectives.

Most importantly, the campaign has laid a strong digital foundation for the next phase. The awareness has been created, the conversations have started and anticipation has been built. The immediate priority is now to convert this growing audience into customers through a well-coordinated product launch supported by conversion-focused campaigns, active community management and continuous performance optimisation. With the momentum already achieved and the quality of engagement recorded, Ikumbi Tea is well positioned to enter the market with an established audience, strong digital visibility and a growing community ready to support the brand.
